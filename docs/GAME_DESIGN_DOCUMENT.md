# Game Design Document (GDD): Real Estate Empire – Africa

## 1. Game Overview
Players start with initial capital in Kampala, Uganda, and build a property development empire through land acquisition, due diligence, construction planning, and rental management.

## 2. Gameplay Loop
- **Acquire**: Explore regional markets (Kampala, Wakiso, Entebbe, etc.), verify land tenure, and purchase land parcels.
- **Develop**: Design and construct residential, commercial, or mixed-use properties.
- **Manage**: Acquire tenants, collect monthly rental income in UGX, and maintain properties.
- **Expand**: Scale operations across Uganda and into neighboring African markets.

## 3. Progression & Economy
Players earn experience points through successful property investments, unlocking higher business titles and larger credit facilities.
