# Uganda Economic & Real Estate Model

## Currency & Inflation
- **Currency**: Ugandan Shilling (UGX).
- **Macroeconomic Baseline**: Bank of Uganda target inflation band (~5.0%), prime lending rates (~17.5%), and annual GDP growth (~6.0%).
- **Land Tenure Systems**: Modeling Mailo land, Freehold, and Leasehold characteristics across Kampala, Wakiso, Entebbe, Mukono, and regional hubs.
