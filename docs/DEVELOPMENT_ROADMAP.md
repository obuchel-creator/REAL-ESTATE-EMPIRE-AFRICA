# Development Roadmap

- **Phase 1 (Current)**: Godot Foundation, Simulation Core, Financial Ledger in UGX, Uganda Starting Profile, Calendar, Save/Load, Automated Tests.
- **Phase 2**: Land Acquisition mechanics, Property Construction workflows, and Tenant Lease management.
- **Phase 3**: Bank Loans, Mortgages, Economic Fluctuations, and Macro-inflation modeling.
- **Phase 4**: Pan-African Expansion into Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania, and Nigeria.
- **Phase 5**: Multi-player Leaderboards and Advanced Urban Planning tools.
